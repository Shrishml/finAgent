## Situation 1: "I have 8 mutual funds and no idea if they overlap"

**The person:** 28-year-old software engineer, ₹50K/month in SIPs across 3 apps (Groww, Coin, direct AMC). Started SIPs over 3 years based on YouTube videos, bank RM suggestions, and Reddit advice.

**What they do today:**
- Logs into 3 apps separately to check performance
- Has no idea that 5 of the 8 funds hold the same top 15 stocks
- Paying expense ratios on all 8 funds for what is essentially 2-3 funds worth of diversification
- Tried Freefincal's overlap tool once — it requires manually entering each fund, doesn't connect to actual holdings, and is a one-time snapshot that goes stale when fund holdings change quarterly

**Why current solutions don't work:**
- Groww/Kuvera show individual fund performance but never compare across your portfolio
- Freefincal overlap tool is manual, one-time, and doesn't show rupee impact
- No app says "these 3 funds are redundant, here's what you're wasting"

**With FinAgent:**
Upload your CAMS statement once. FinAgent instantly maps every fund's holdings, builds an overlap matrix, and tells you: *"Funds A, C, and E have 72% stock overlap. You're paying ₹11,400/year in duplicate expense ratios. Consolidate into Fund A alone — same exposure, one-third the cost. Here's a tax-optimized exit plan for C and E."*
