# FinAgent Naming Decision

**Date:** April 3, 2026
**Final Choice:** `finbestie` (finbestie.com)
**Status:** Locked in ✅

---

## Decision Summary

After evaluating 100+ name candidates across 5 rounds, we selected **finbestie** — "finance + bestie" — as the product name. It's casual, globally understood, and perfectly aligned with our Gen Z/millennial target audience.

## Evaluation Rounds

### Round 1: Meaningful Short Names (Fyno/Lucre style)
Checked 30 names across .com/.ai/.dev/.io.

**Best finds:**
- **rivvest** — all 4 TLDs open, "river + invest". Strong but generic.
- **coinlens** — all 4 TLDs open, "coin + lens". Crypto-adjacent connotation was a concern.
- **myntro** (.ai available) — "mint + retro", catchy but invented.
- **welvio** (.ai available) — sounds premium but no inherent meaning.

**Rejected because:** Most short meaningful English words are taken on .com. Invented words lacked instant recognition.

### Round 2: Funky/Meaningless Names
Checked 40 names — absurdist, meme-culture, no finance connection.

**Best finds:**
- **razzl** (.ai/.dev/.io) — fun to say, razzle-dazzle energy.
- **plynx** (.ai/.dev/.io) — unique, sharp sound.
- **velvetbuzz** — all 4 TLDs open, but long for a "short name" preference.

**Rejected because:** No connection to product purpose. Hard to build brand story around meaningless names.

### Round 3: Longer Funky Names
Checked 35 names — compound words, playful combos.

**Best finds:**
- **velvetbuzz** (.com available!) — smooth + electric.
- **thundermint** (.ai/.dev/.io) — powerful + fresh.
- **turbopanda** (.ai/.dev/.io) — instant personality.

**Rejected because:** Too playful for a finance product. Could undermine trust.

### Round 4: Hindu Mythology Names
Checked 30 names — direct mythology and mythology-modern blends.

**Best finds (pure mythology):**
- **koshagara** — all 4 TLDs, "royal treasurer" in Sanskrit.
- **chintamani** — "wish-fulfilling jewel", beautiful and mystical.
- **navnidhi** — all 4 TLDs, "nine treasures of Kubera".

**Best finds (mythology + modern blends):**
- **kuberaai** — all 4 TLDs, "god of wealth + AI".
- **kalpiq** — all 4 TLDs, "cosmic era + IQ", only 6 letters.
- **devadhan** — all 4 TLDs, "divine wealth".
- **dhanlens** — all 4 TLDs, "see your wealth clearly".

**Rejected because:** Non-Hindi speakers wouldn't understand the meaning. Limits global appeal despite deep cultural resonance for Indian market.

### Round 5: User-Proposed Names (Final)
Suraj proposed two names:

| Name | Meaning | .com | .ai | .dev | .io | .app |
|------|---------|------|-----|------|-----|------|
| **finbestie** | finance + bestie | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **arthamitr** | artha (wealth) + mitr (friend) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

Both fully available across all 5 TLDs.

## Why finbestie Won

| Criteria | finbestie | arthamitr |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Global appeal | ✅ Instantly understood | ⚠️ Needs explanation outside India |
| Target audience (Gen Z) | 🎯 "Bestie" is their language | Warm but traditional |
| Memorability | High — two common words | Medium — unfamiliar to non-Hindi speakers |
| SEO searchability | Easy — "fin bestie" | Harder globally |
| Tone | Casual, approachable | More premium/traditional |
| Brand ceiling | Could feel casual at scale | Ages better |
| Pronunciation | Universal | Non-Indians may stumble |

**Decision rationale:** Global product with Gen Z/millennial target audience needs instant recognition in every market. "Bestie" is universally understood internet slang. The tagline writes itself: "Your finance bestie."

**arthamitr** preserved as potential Hindi tagline for India beachhead: "FinBestie — आपका अर्थमित्र"
