Last week, a friend told me he finally sat down and calculated the actual return on his LIC policy.

The agent had sold it as "3x maturity in 20 years." Sounds great, right? Triple your money.

Except when you do the math, that's a CAGR of ~6.5%.

His home loan interest rate? 8.5%.

He's been paying 8.5% on debt while his "investment" earns 6.5%. For years. Nobody told him. The agent certainly didn't. The policy document didn't make it obvious. And no app he uses shows this side by side.

His words stuck with me: "Agents tell us what we want to hear, not what's actually happening."

That one conversation sent me down a rabbit hole. I spent weeks talking to friends, reading hundreds of posts on finance forums. The same patterns kept showing up:

— Someone discovers ₹48K in hidden expense charges on their mutual funds. Had no idea.
— Someone has 15 funds accumulated over years. Paralyzed. Can't figure out which to sell without a tax calculator nobody has.
— Someone's "guaranteed return" plan is quietly losing to inflation.

The math isn't hard. XIRR, CAGR, expense ratios, overlap analysis — these have correct answers. But the people selling you products have zero incentive to show you.

So I'm building FinAgent — an open-source AI agent that just shows you the real numbers. No commissions. No affiliate links. Your data stays on your machine.

It's early. Very early. But I pinged 12 friends with the idea — 9 said "I need this." Every conversation ended the same way: "Why doesn't this exist already?"

Good question. Building it now.
