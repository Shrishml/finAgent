The Math Nobody Shows You

Here's what happens to ₹10 lakh in two instruments, assuming the 30% tax bracket:

Difference: ₹14,000/year. Same liquidity. Same risk profile. Just better tax treatment.

Why This Happens

  • FD interest is taxed as income — at your marginal rate (up to 30% + cess)
  • TDS adds friction — bank deducts 10% TDS, you claim it back while filing, cash flow disrupted
  • Arbitrage funds get equity taxation — LTCG at 12.5% after 1 year, significantly lower
  • Most people don't know alternatives exist — banks certainly won't tell you
