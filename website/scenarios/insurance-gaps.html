The Hidden Gaps in Your Policy

Most people buy health insurance based on premium and sum insured. But the fine print has clauses that can reduce your effective coverage by 50% or more:

Room Rent Cap
Policy says ₹5,000/day. Private hospital rooms in Bangalore or Mumbai cost ₹8,000–15,000/day. You pay the difference.
Copay Clause
20% copay on claims above ₹2L means you pay ₹60,000 out of pocket on a ₹5L surgery.
Sub-Limits
Cataract surgery capped at ₹40,000 when actual cost is ₹80,000+. The "₹10L cover" doesn't mean ₹10L for everything.
Non-Disclosure
Forgot to mention a condition you didn't think was relevant? Entire claim rejected — even years later.
