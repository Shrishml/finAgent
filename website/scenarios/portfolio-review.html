The Problem

Every week, investors post their full portfolio on Reddit asking strangers to review it. The responses?

What happens today

Common variations we see constantly:

  • "I have 7 mutual funds — am I over-diversified?"

  • "Should I consolidate from 8 funds to 4?"

How FinAgent Solves This

Step 1: Upload your CAS statement

Your Consolidated Account Statement from CAMS/KFintech has every mutual fund you own. One upload gives FinAgent your complete picture.

Step 2: Overlap matrix

FinAgent checks the actual stock holdings of each fund. "Fund A and Fund B have 82% overlap — you're paying two expense ratios for essentially the same portfolio."

Step 3: Expense ratio audit

Step 4: Specific recommendations
